MAP The Prompt
Prompt
I'm a [your role] about to [your situation]. I'm smart but not an expert in [this domain]. Give me a five-minute executive briefing, written at a 5th grade reading level, using simple sentences and words: → Key concepts and terms I need to know → The major options or approaches and how they differ → How this domain typically works — the "normal" path → What outsiders consistently get wrong or misunderstand
↑ Use the highest reasoning model available: Claude Opus 4.6 · GPT 5.2 · Gemini 3 Pro

Phase 2
The Inversion Principle

Don't ask how to succeed.
Ask what already went wrong.

📈
"How do I succeed?"
What most people ask
vs
🔍
"Imagine this already failed — what went wrong?"
The pre-mortem approach
MINE The Prompt
Prompt
Good. Now go deeper. Given my specific situation… [dictate your budget, timeline, constraints, what you're trying to achieve] …what are the hidden complexities most people in my position don't realize until it's too late? Be specific: → What seems simple but is actually tricky? → What are the most expensive mistakes people make? → What second-order effects should I be thinking about? → What would a twenty-year veteran tell me over coffee?
🎙 Dictate your context — don't type it. More details = more specific output.

MOVE The Prompt
Prompt
Based on everything we've discussed, I need to be ready to [your specific action — have a meeting, make a decision, start a project]. Given the context I shared, give me: → The three to five most important questions I should ask → The specific information I should gather before proceeding → The smartest first step given my situation → Red flags to watch for once I start
